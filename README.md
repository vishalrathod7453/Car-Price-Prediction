## 📌 Project Overview

Buying or selling a used car can be challenging because determining the right market price is not always straightforward.

This project leverages **Machine Learning** to predict the selling price of a used car based on multiple vehicle attributes such as:

* 📅 Manufacturing Year
* ⛽ Fuel Type
* 🚘 Transmission Type
* 📍 Kilometers Driven
* 👤 Number of Previous Owners
* 🏢 Seller Type
* 💰 Current Ex-Showroom Price

The model analyzes historical vehicle data and provides an estimated selling price with high accuracy.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Used car prices vary significantly depending on vehicle condition, age, ownership history, and market demand.

Manual price estimation often results in:

❌ Overpricing

❌ Underpricing

❌ Market uncertainty

❌ Loss of profit

This AI-powered solution helps buyers and sellers make data-driven pricing decisions.
